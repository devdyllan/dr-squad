---

## 1. Business Context

We are building a nutraceutical direct-response business targeting consumers in the **DACH region and Luxembourg** — specifically Germany (DE), Austria (AT), Switzerland (CH), and Luxembourg (LU).

We operate in **two niches simultaneously:**
- **Weight Loss (WL)** — supplements, protocols, body recomposition
- **Erectile Dysfunction (ED)** — performance, men's health, vitality

Both niches target the nutraceutical category: non-prescription supplements sold direct-to-consumer, primarily via paid social media (Meta) and potentially native advertising.

**We are pre-revenue. No products are live. No campaigns are running.**

---

## 2. Current Phase: Research

Before building copy, creative, offers, or campaigns, we need market and consumer intelligence. The Research Squad is the first active squad. All other squads are on standby until research deliverables are complete.

**Research Phase Objective:**
> Understand who is buying weight loss and ED supplements in the DACH/LU market, what they believe, what they fear, what they've tried, what language they use, and who is winning market share right now.

---

## 3. Research Mandate — What Must Be Delivered

The CEO Agent is directed to issue the following Task Packets to the Research Chief immediately upon receipt of this Brief.

### Deliverable 1: Market Intelligence Brief (MIB) — DACH/LU Nutraceutical
**What it answers:**
- What is the size and growth trajectory of the weight loss and ED supplement market in DE, AT, CH, LU?
- Which traffic channels are dominant for nutraceutical DTC brands in these markets?
- What regulatory environment governs health claims in DE/AT/CH/LU? (What can and cannot be said in ads?)
- What are the dominant consumer purchase behaviors — marketplace vs. DTC vs. pharmacy?
- What seasonal or cultural factors affect buying patterns in this region?

### Deliverable 2: Buyer Psychology Profile (BPP) — Weight Loss / DACH/LU
**What it answers:**
- Who is the WL supplement buyer in this market? Demographics, psychographics, lifestyle.
- What is their internal narrative about their weight? What do they believe is the cause?
- What have they already tried? What failed and why (in their own words)?
- What would "working" look like to them — what outcome do they want?
- What are their top objections to buying another supplement?
- What words and phrases do they actually use to describe their problem?

### Deliverable 3: Buyer Psychology Profile (BPP) — Erectile Dysfunction / DACH/LU
**What it answers:**
- Who is the ED supplement buyer in this market? Age range, relationship status, self-perception.
- How do they frame the problem? (Health issue? Aging? Stress? Relationship issue?)
- What's the shame and stigma profile? What do they admit vs. hide?
- What have they tried? (Prescription vs. OTC vs. lifestyle vs. nothing)
- What triggers purchase intent — fear, hope, frustration, relationship pressure?
- What language do they use in private (forums, anonymous posts) vs. public?

### Deliverable 4: Competitive Intelligence Report (CIR) — WL and ED Nutraceuticals in DACH/LU
**What it answers:**
- Who are the top 5–10 advertisers in each niche in this market? (Facebook Ad Library)
- What angles and mechanisms are they leading with?
- What creatives are running longest (high longevity = working)?
- What offers and price points are dominant?
- What landing page structures and VSL formats are they using?
- Where are the gaps — angles no one is running, objections no one is handling?

---

## 4. Research Constraints — Non-Negotiable

| Constraint | Rule |
|---|---|
| **$0 research budget** | No paid tools. No data purchases. No panel surveys. No API costs for data. |
| **Free sources only** | Facebook Ad Library, YouTube (comments + content), Reddit, Instagram, German/Austrian/Swiss consumer forums and health blogs, Google Search (manual), Amazon reviews (for similar products in market), Trustpilot/Trustpilot.de reviews |
| **Primary language priority** | German content is primary. French content for LU/CH. English as secondary signal source only. |
| **No extrapolation from US data** | DACH buyer behavior, health culture, and regulatory context differ significantly from US markets. US sources may not be used as primary evidence for DACH market claims. |
| **Evidence over assumption** | Every BPP claim must trace to a specific source. No "consumers typically feel..." without a source. |

Upon research phase completion, the Owner will review and approve:

The Dev Squad, Ads Squad, and VSL Squad remain on standby until after Copy Phase.

---

## 8. Owner Directives

1. **Research first, everything else second.** No copy, no creative, no offers until BPPs and MIB are delivered.
2. **Free sources only.** If the Research Chief identifies a paid data source that would meaningfully accelerate research quality, they escalate to CEO Agent who escalates to Owner. Owner decides. Default is no.
3. **German-market specificity is required.** General European health marketing insights are not sufficient. Research must be specific to DACH/LU consumer behavior.
4. **Notify Owner via WhatsApp when each deliverable is complete.** CEO Agent delivers a brief summary and the research quality score. Owner reviews before phase transition.
5. **No campaigns, no ad spend, no infrastructure building during research phase.** The Contingency Squad, Ads Squad, and VSL Squad do not activate until Owner issues a new Strategic Direction Brief authorizing the next phase.
