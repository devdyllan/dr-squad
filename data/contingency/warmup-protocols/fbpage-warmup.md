# Facebook Page Creation + Warmup Protocol
**Protocol ID:** WUP-FBPAGE-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Applies to:** FBPAGE_NUTRA_01, FBPAGE_NUTRA_02
**Maintained by:** Contingency Squad
**Environment:** NON-PRODUCTION / CONTINGENCY ONLY

---

## Purpose

Facebook Pages must be created by warmed profiles and maintained with basic organic activity before being used for advertising. A cold page with no posts, no followers, and no engagement that runs ads is immediately flagged as low-quality.

---

## Pre-Requisites

- Admin FBPRO must have completed 7+ days of warmup (WUP-FBPRO-001)
- Pre-Session Isolation Certificate: CLEAR
- No ad campaigns will be created in this phase
- No ad budget will be attached

---

## Day 5 — Page Creation

**Executor:** Asset Farming Operator

```
Step 1: Open BROWSER_META_0X (admin FBPRO profile)
Step 2: Navigate to facebook.com/pages/create
Step 3: Create page with:
  - Category: Health/Beauty or Product/Service (general — not ad-specific)
  - Name: [Placeholder brand name — Owner to confirm, or use generic health brand name]
  - Page type: Business or Brand
Step 4: Upload a simple profile image (placeholder — no branding needed yet)
Step 5: Add basic bio text (generic health/wellness description)
Step 6: Do NOT publish any posts yet
Step 7: Log Page ID in asset-registry.yaml
```

---

## Days 5–14 — Page Organic Warmup

**Sessions:** 3 posts per week minimum
**Content type:** General health/wellness content — share public articles, motivational posts, informational content in German

### Week 1 (Days 5–11):
- [ ] Post 2–3 times (share public health articles or generic wellness tips)
- [ ] Posts should be in German (primary) or neutral
- [ ] Do NOT use any product claims or supplement mentions yet
- [ ] Respond to any organic comments if they appear

### Week 2 (Days 12–14):
- [ ] Continue posting 2–3 times
- [ ] Page should have 5+ posts before any ad activity
- [ ] Invite friends to like the page (optional — only if organic reach desired)

---

## Readiness Criteria

Page is READY when:
- [ ] Page is 7+ days old
- [ ] Has 5+ posts with organic content
- [ ] No policy warnings from Meta on any post
- [ ] Admin FBPRO account in good standing
- [ ] Pre-Session Isolation Certificate CLEAR

---

## Constraints

- No boosted posts
- No "Promote Page" actions
- No ad account linked until Owner authorization
- No product-specific health claims in posts (regulatory risk)
