> **Buyer Proxy opening note:**
> The ED buyer rarely says "I want better erections" in public channels. He says "I have stress," "I am exhausted," or "it works sometimes." The purchase trigger is often not libido. It is fear of losing identity, confidence, and relationship stability.

---

## Section 1 — Profile Summary

The core ED supplement buyer in DACH/LU is predominantly male, usually 35–65, and split into two psychological groups:

- **Group A (35–49):** frames the issue as stress, performance anxiety, sleep, and pressure
- **Group B (50–65):** frames the issue as aging, blood flow, hormones, and loss of masculinity status

He usually does not start with supplements. He first tries silence, avoidance, and self-correction (sleep, alcohol reduction, gym), then private search behavior, then either OTC/supplement experimentation or a physician route. In DACH, trust in medical authority is high, but shame creates delay.

The strongest purchasing friction is not price. It is credibility plus privacy.

## Section 3 — Problem Framing (How He Names It)

Vocabulary appears on a clinical-to-private spectrum:

- **Clinical/public:** `Erektile Dysfunktion`, `Erektionsstörung`
- **Common/private:** `Erektionsprobleme`, `Potenzprobleme`
- **Euphemistic/private:** `es klappt nicht`, `nicht hart genug`, `es hält nicht`

Dominant frames:
1. "This is stress/anxiety" (especially <50)
2. "This is circulation/age" (especially 50+)
3. "This is both physical and psychological" (after repeated failures)

In anonymous channels, language is explicit and distress-heavy. In semi-public channels, men code-switch to softer language.

## Section 4 — What He Already Tried

### Typical sequence before supplement purchase
1. waits and hopes it resolves
2. searches anonymously (forums/Reddit/YouTube comments)
3. tries self-directed fixes (sleep, exercise, reduce porn/alcohol)
4. tests OTC/supplement products (L-Arginine, Maca, Ginseng blends)
5. considers physician route or PDE5 path (Sildenafil/Tadalafil)

### Reported failures
- inconsistent effect ("works sometimes")
- headaches/flushing fear from stronger options
- distrust of "miracle" product pages
- fear of non-discreet shipping and billing labels

### Supplement ingredient awareness (DACH channels)
- L-Arginin / Citrullin (blood-flow framing)
- Maca (libido/vitality framing)
- Panax Ginseng (energy + vitality framing)
- Zinc / Selenium / Vitamin D (male-health baseline framing)

## Section 5 — Desired Outcome

What "working" means:
- reliability in intimate context
- reduced anticipatory anxiety
- feeling "normal" again, not "superhuman"
- minimal side effects
- discreet process from purchase to delivery

He is more receptive to "restore reliability" than "extreme performance" promises.

---

## Section 6 — Purchase Triggers

Primary triggers:
1. recent failed intimate event
2. partner conversation or relationship tension
3. visible persistence of issue over weeks/months
4. ad/message that combines mechanism + privacy + no-shame tone
5. clear guarantee and discreet fulfillment

### Buyer intent moments (high signal language)
- "Ich will endlich wieder Sicherheit haben."
- "Ich brauche etwas, das zuverlässig funktioniert."
- "Ich will nicht jedes Mal Angst davor haben."

## Section 7 — Objections and Barriers

Top objections ranked:

1. **"Wahrscheinlich Scam / nur Marketing."** (HIGH)
2. **"Wirkt das wirklich oder ist es Placebo?"** (HIGH)
3. **"Sieht jemand das Paket / die Abbuchung?"** (HIGH)
4. **"Ist das mit meinem Blutdruck/Medikamenten sicher?"** (MEDIUM-HIGH)
5. **"Ich will nicht dauerhaft abhängig sein."** (MEDIUM)

### Barrier dynamics
- High shame increases delay and lowers direct help-seeking.
- High privacy concern increases preference for anonymous browsing and discreet checkout.
- High medical caution in DACH increases demand for credible, non-exaggerated mechanism language.

---

## Section 8 — Trust Signals

Strongest trust signals in this niche:

1. clear ingredient + plausible mechanism (not magical language)
2. compliance-safe language (no disease-cure grandstanding)
3. German quality/manufacturing and transparent labeling
4. discreet shipping + discreet billing descriptor
5. realistic promise + money-back guarantee

Fast distrust triggers:
- "instant miracle" tone
- fake doctor persona or unverifiable endorsements
- unclear ingredient dosing
- aggressive masculinity shaming
