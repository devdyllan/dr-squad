## Objective

Produce a Market Intelligence Brief covering the nutraceutical supplement market in Germany (DE), Austria (AT), Switzerland (CH), and Luxembourg (LU) — with specific focus on the weight loss and erectile dysfunction supplement categories.

**Deliverable:** `data/research/outputs/MIB-001_DACH-LU-NUTRACEUTICAL.md`
**Quality Gate:** RQS ≥ 75 before CEO Agent review

---

## Questions This Research Must Answer

### Market Structure
- What is the approximate market size and growth trajectory for WL and ED supplements in DACH/LU? (Even directional signals are acceptable — exact figures are not required if unavailable from free sources.)
- Which distribution channels dominate — DTC/direct website, marketplace (Amazon.de), pharmacy (Apotheke), or subscription model?
- Is DTC supplement advertising on Meta (Facebook/Instagram) active and common in these markets? Which brands are running at scale?

### Traffic and Media Landscape
- Which ad platforms do nutraceutical brands use to reach DACH/LU buyers? (Meta, YouTube, native, programmatic?)
- What traffic patterns are visible in the Facebook Ad Library for WL and ED supplement brands targeting DE/AT/CH/LU?
- Are VSLs (video sales letters) present in this market, or are static image ads and short-form video dominant?

### Regulatory Context
- What health claim restrictions apply to supplement advertising in DE, AT, CH, and LU?
- What language is prohibited or restricted in supplement ads in these markets? (EU health claim regulations vs. national specifics)
- What compliance patterns do successful advertisers use — i.e., how do they make compelling claims without triggering platform or regulatory issues?

### Consumer Behavior
- Is there a dominant purchase trigger (fear of aging, doctor recommendation, social proof, price)?
- Are DACH consumers skeptical of supplements? What trust signals are most visible in successful brands?
- Is subscription (Abo-Modell) common and accepted, or is single-purchase the norm?

### Seasonal and Cultural Factors
- Are there known seasonal peaks for WL supplements (e.g., January/Neujahr, pre-Sommer)?
- Are there known seasonal peaks for ED/men's health supplements?
- Are there cultural factors specific to German-speaking markets that affect health product buying behavior?
