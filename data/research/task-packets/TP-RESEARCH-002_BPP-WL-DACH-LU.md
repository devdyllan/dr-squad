## Objective

Build a deep, evidence-based buyer psychology profile for the weight loss supplement buyer in the DACH/LU market (DE, AT, CH, LU). This profile will directly inform every piece of messaging, copy, and creative produced by the Copy Squad and VSL Squad.

**The standard for quality:** Every claim in this BPP must trace to a specific, citable, real source — a comment, a review, a forum post, a YouTube comment, a Reddit thread. This is not a generalized marketing persona. It is a profile of what real people in this market actually say, feel, and believe.

**Deliverable:** `data/research/outputs/BPP-001_WL-DACH-LU.md`
**Quality Gate:** RQS ≥ 75 before CEO Agent review

## Profile Dimensions — What Must Be Researched

### 1. Demographics and Life Context
- Age range of active buyers (where possible from reviews, social profiles, comment patterns)
- Gender split — is this market female-skewed, male-skewed, or mixed?
- Life stage — parents with young children? Middle-aged professionals? Retirees?
- Geographic distribution — are there differences between DE, AT, CH, and LU buyer profiles?

### 2. Internal Narrative — How They See Their Problem
- How do they describe their weight problem in their own words? (German/French sources)
- Do they attribute it to: genetics, stress, age, lifestyle, hormones, metabolism? What is the dominant frame?
- How long have they had the problem? Is this a chronic struggle or a recent trigger?
- What is the emotional relationship with the problem — shame? Frustration? Resignation? Motivation?

### 3. Failed Solutions — What They've Already Tried
- Which diets have they tried? (Keto, IF, calorie counting, Atkins, Mediterranean — which are most referenced in DACH contexts?)
- Which supplements or products have they previously purchased?
- Why did previous solutions fail? (In their words — was it discipline, results, cost, side effects?)
- How does previous failure affect their willingness to try again?

### 4. Desired Outcome
- What does "success" look like in their mind? (A number on the scale? Fitting into old clothes? Energy levels? Social confidence?)
- What is the minimum acceptable result they'd consider "worth it"?
- How long are they willing to wait for results?

### 5. Purchase Triggers
- What causes them to buy? (Doctor recommendation? Seeing before/after? Running out of patience? Social event approaching?)
- What is the "last straw" moment that typically precedes a purchase decision?
- Does a partner's comment, a photo, or a health scare drive purchases more than personal motivation?

### 6. Objections and Barriers
- What makes them hesitate before buying a supplement? (Skepticism about efficacy? Past bad experiences? Price? Ingredient concerns?)
- What specific phrases do they use when expressing doubt?
- What would make them feel "safe" enough to buy?

### 7. Trust Signals
- What makes them trust a brand or product? (Doctor recommendation? Scientific studies? Before/after photos? Money-back guarantee? German quality signals?)
- Which influencer or authority types do they trust in the health space?
- What makes them distrust a product immediately?

### 8. Language Profile
- Compile 20–30 verbatim quotes in German (and French for LU/CH) from real sources — actual words people use to describe their problem, their attempts, and their frustration
- Note: which words appear most frequently in positive reviews of WL supplements?
- Note: which words appear most frequently in negative reviews / complaints?

## Constraints

- **$0 budget.** Free sources only.
- **Direct quotes required.** The BPP must include verbatim quotes in German (and/or French) from real sources. No paraphrasing without attribution.
- **No US market data as primary evidence.** DACH/LU consumer context is distinct.
- **Do not import American buyer psychology frameworks without verification that they apply to this market.**

## Deliverable Format

Standard BPP template. Key sections:

1. **Profile Summary** — one-paragraph portrait of the WL supplement buyer in DACH/LU
2. **Demographics and Life Context**
3. **Internal Narrative** — how they frame their problem
4. **Failed Solutions** — what they've tried and why it didn't work
5. **Desired Outcome** — what winning looks like to them
6. **Purchase Triggers** — what causes them to buy
7. **Objections and Barriers** — what causes them to hesitate
8. **Trust Signals** — what makes them feel safe buying
9. **Verbatim Language Bank** — 20–30 real quotes in German/French, categorized by theme
10. **Research Quality Assessment** — sources, confidence levels, gaps
11. **Evidence Appendix** — source citations for every claim

**RQS ≥ 75 required.** If gaps remain after exhausting free sources, the Research Chief flags the specific gap and its potential impact on messaging quality.
