## Objective

Build a deep, evidence-based buyer psychology profile for the erectile dysfunction supplement buyer in the DACH/LU market (DE, AT, CH, LU). This is a sensitive niche with significant shame dynamics, code-switching behavior (what buyers say publicly vs. privately), and specific cultural attitudes in German-speaking markets that cannot be assumed from other markets.

**The standard for quality:** Every claim must trace to a specific, citable, real source. This BPP must capture what real men in this market actually write, believe, and feel — not what a marketer assumes they feel.

**Deliverable:** `data/research/outputs/BPP-002_ED-DACH-LU.md`
**Quality Gate:** RQS ≥ 75 before CEO Agent review

## Profile Dimensions — What Must Be Researched

### 1. Demographics and Life Context
- What age ranges are most actively discussing or seeking ED supplement solutions? (Note: the visible buyer may skew older but search data and forum activity may reveal a wider range)
- Relationship status signals — are buyers primarily in long-term relationships experiencing performance anxiety, or singles?
- Are there signals about professional/lifestyle context — stress, sedentary work, travel, alcohol use, referenced as contributing factors?

### 2. Problem Framing — How They Describe It
- Do they call it "Erektionsproblem"? "Erektionsstörung"? "Potenzproblem"? Map the exact language spectrum from clinical to casual.
- Do they frame it as: a medical issue, an aging issue, a temporary stress issue, a relationship problem, a confidence issue? What is the dominant frame in DACH sources?
- How long have they typically had the issue before seeking a supplement? Is this a recent trigger or a long-standing chronic issue?
- Is there a shame gradient — do they talk about it openly in some forums and use heavy code or anonymity in others?

### 3. What They've Already Tried
- Have they consulted a doctor? (German healthcare culture tends toward medical consultation — what percentage seem to have gone to a Urologe or general practitioner first?)
- Are they already aware of pharmaceutical options (Viagra/Sildenafil, Cialis/Tadalafil)? What's their attitude — rejected them? Can't afford? Don't want prescription?
- What supplement brands or ingredients have they already tried? (Maca, L-Arginin, Tribulus, Ginseng — what is the DACH supplement landscape in this niche?)
- Why did previous solutions fail? (Didn't work, side effects, cost, embarrassment at pharmacy?)

### 4. Desired Outcome
- What does success look like? Is it clinical (full function return)? Situational (work reliably with partner)? Confidence-based (feel like themselves again)?
- Is there a desire to avoid ever needing a prescription — i.e., supplement as "natural" solution?
- What is the relationship between this problem and their sense of masculinity or self-worth? How explicit are they about this in sources?

### 5. Purchase Triggers
- What causes them to finally act? (Partner brings it up? Relationship in danger? Upcoming romantic situation? Age milestone?)
- Is this a planned purchase or an impulse / desperation purchase?
- What is the typical research behavior before purchase — do they research extensively or buy quickly from the first credible-seeming option?

### 6. Objections and Barriers
- What specific fears stop them from buying? (Waste of money? Doesn't work? Ingredients unsafe? Arrives at home and is seen?)
- Is delivery privacy a major objection in this market? (Discreet packaging references in reviews?)
- What are the fraud/scam concerns specific to this category?
- Does stigma prevent them from buying even when they want to?

### 7. Trust Signals
- What makes an ED supplement brand credible to a German-market buyer? (Clinical language? German manufacturing? Doctor endorsement? Money-back guarantee? Natural ingredients emphasis?)
- Is "Made in Germany" / "Hergestellt in Deutschland" or "Qualität aus Deutschland" a meaningful trust signal in this specific category?
- What makes them immediately distrust a product?

### 8. Language Profile — CRITICAL for This Niche
- Verbatim quotes in German (and French for LU/CH): 20–30 real quotes from forums, reviews, and comment sections
- Map the full vocabulary spectrum: clinical terms → common terms → slang/euphemisms used in anonymous contexts
- Note: What terms appear in negative/frustrated contexts vs. hopeful/positive contexts?
- Note: What phrases signal highest purchase intent ("ich habe es endlich probiert", "nach Jahren endlich", etc.)?

## Research Sources

**Note:** This niche requires access to more private/anonymous discussion venues. Men in DACH markets discussing ED are significantly more likely to use anonymous forums and pseudonymous accounts. The Research Chief should prioritize sources where anonymity is present — this is where the real buyer psychology lives.

| Source | What to Extract |
|---|---|
| Reddit: r/de, r/Austria, r/Switzerland, r/Beziehungsrat, r/maennlichkeit | Anonymous threads about ED — real language, real fears, real experiences |
| German anonymous health forums | gutefrage.net threads on Erektionsproblem/Potenzproblem, urologie-forum.de, männergesundheit forums |
| Amazon.de / Amazon.at ED supplement reviews | 1–4 star reviews on top-selling ED supplements — what language do buyers use? What failed? What worked? |
| YouTube comments (DE) | Comments on videos about ED causes, natural treatments, men's health — what do men ask? What do they admit? |
| Facebook Ad Library (DE/AT/CH/LU) | ED supplement ads running in these markets — which brands, what angles, what longevity |
| Trustpilot.de | ED supplement brand reviews — full vocabulary mining |
| Instagram — private hashtag and comment exploration | Less likely to surface private content but brand pages and comment sections may reveal language |

**Warning:** Treat all quotes as research material with respect for privacy. Do not reproduce personally identifying information. Quote the emotional/linguistic content, not identifying details.

## Constraints

- **$0 budget.** Free sources only.
- **No US market data as primary evidence.** German attitudes toward health, masculinity, and pharmaceutical culture differ materially from US context.
- **Direct quotes required** — German/French verbatim language is essential for this BPP.
- **Shame dynamics must be documented.** If the research shows that buyers use anonymity heavily, the BPP must document this — it directly affects copy tone and offer structure.

## Deliverable Format

Standard BPP template. Key sections:

1. **Profile Summary** — one-paragraph portrait of the ED supplement buyer in DACH/LU
2. **Demographics and Life Context**
3. **Problem Framing** — how they describe and name the issue
4. **Prior Solutions** — what they've tried; pharmaceutical awareness and attitude

6. **Purchase Triggers**

8. **Trust Signals**
9. **Shame and Anonymity Map** — where on the public/private spectrum does this buyer operate?
10. **Verbatim Language Bank** — 20–30 quotes in German/French, categorized
11. **Research Quality Assessment**
12. **Evidence Appendix**

**RQS ≥ 75 required.** If the shame dynamic makes public source mining insufficient, the Research Chief flags the gap explicitly — this is important strategic information for the Copy Squad.
