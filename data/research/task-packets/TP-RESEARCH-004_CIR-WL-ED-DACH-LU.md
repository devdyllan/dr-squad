## Objective

Map the competitive landscape for weight loss and erectile dysfunction nutraceutical brands actively advertising in Germany (DE), Austria (AT), Switzerland (CH), and Luxembourg (LU). Identify which brands are winning, what angles they're running, how long their ads have been live (longevity = signal of profitability), and where the gaps are that we can enter.

**This is a market intelligence mission, not an inspiration gathering exercise.** The goal is not to replicate competitors. It is to understand the battlefield so we can find an uncontested position.

**Deliverable:** `data/research/outputs/CIR-001_WL-ED-DACH-LU.md`
**Quality Gate:** RQS ≥ 75 before CEO Agent review

## Competitive Intelligence Dimensions

### Section 1: Advertiser Mapping (Facebook Ad Library)

For both WL and ED niches, using Facebook Ad Library with country filter set to DE, AT, CH, and LU:

**For each active advertiser found:**
- Brand name and URL
- Number of active ads (scale signal)
- Approximate ad run duration (date first seen — longevity signal)
- Primary ad formats used (video, image, carousel)
- Primary languages used in ads (German, French, English)
- Geographic targeting visible in disclaimers or ad copy

**Minimum targets:**
- WL niche: Identify top 8–10 advertisers with sustained ad presence (running 30+ days)
- ED niche: Identify top 5–8 advertisers with sustained ad presence

### Section 2: Angle and Mechanism Analysis

For each identified advertiser, analyze:

**Primary angle:** What is the lead hook? Examples:
- Fear-based (fear of permanent damage, fear of relationship failure)
- Mechanism-based (proprietary ingredient, clinical study, German formula)
- Story-based (doctor discovers, husband transforms)
- Social proof (10,000 German men have used)
- Urgency-based (limited stock, seasonal offer)

**Core mechanism claim:** What does the product claim to do and why?
- Does it cite an ingredient mechanism? (e.g., "L-Arginin erhöht die Durchblutung")
- Does it reference a study or clinical data?
- Does it invoke a deficiency frame? (e.g., "nach 40 fehlt Ihnen...")
- Does it use a natural/vs-pharmaceutical contrast?

**Target buyer signal:** What buyer profile does this ad seem to be targeting based on copy, imagery, and messaging?

### Section 3: Creative Format Analysis

- What creative formats dominate for each niche — long-form video (VSL), short-form video (15–30s), static image, UGC-style?
- Are talking-head videos common? Doctor testimonials? Before/after imagery?
- What is the typical CTA — "Jetzt bestellen", "Mehr erfahren", "Zum Angebot"?
- Are there advertorial-style landing pages (disguised as editorial content)?

### Section 4: Offer and Price Architecture

Where visible (from landing pages, ad copy, or public product pages):
- What price points are common for WL supplements in DACH? Single bottle vs. bundle?
- What price points are common for ED supplements?
- Is subscription (Abo) prominent, or single-purchase dominant?
- What risk-reversal offers are used — money-back guarantee duration? Free trial?
- What upsell or bundle structures are common?

### Section 5: Landing Page and Funnel Patterns

For top 3–5 advertisers per niche:
- Does the ad click to: a direct product page, a VSL page, a quiz funnel, an advertorial?
- What is the headline structure on the landing page?
- Is there a VSL? Approximate length if detectable?
- What social proof elements are used — reviews, certifications, media mentions?
- Are there urgency or scarcity elements?

### Section 6: Gap Analysis — What No One Is Doing

After mapping the competitive landscape:
- Which buyer objections are being ignored by current advertisers?
- Which buyer segments are not being directly addressed?
- Which angles have not appeared in the research?
- Which product mechanisms are over-claimed (worn out) vs. under-claimed (opportunity)?
- Is there a creative format not in use that the market might respond to?
- Is there a trust signal no one is using that DACH buyers specifically value?

## Primary Research Source: Facebook Ad Library

**Access:** facebook.com/ads/library — FREE, no login required for basic access.

**Search protocol:**
1. Set country: Germany (DE) — primary
2. Set category: All Ads
3. Search: "[brand name]" or keyword searches like "abnehmen supplement", "Gewichtsverlust", "Potenz", "Erektion", "Männergesundheit"
4. Filter: Active ads only first; then expand to All to see recent/expired ads
5. Sort by: Active ads, note "started running on" date for longevity assessment
6. Repeat for: AT (Austria), CH (Switzerland), LU (Luxembourg)

**Also search:**
- Competitor brand names directly once discovered
- Product ingredient names ("L-Carnitin Abnehmen", "L-Arginin Potenz")
- German diet/supplement brand names: XLS-Medical, WeightWorld, Almased, Yokebe (known WL brands) as anchor points to discover adjacent advertisers
